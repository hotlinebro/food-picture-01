# Tonkotsu Noodle Pull — Round 3 (Steamlit)

A photorealistic steaming tonkotsu ramen, steam-lit and moody:
chopsticks lifting a tall glossy noodle pull out of creamy broth, a
halved jammy soft-boiled egg with a runny golden yolk, green onions,
nori, and a speckled ceramic bowl, with warm steam catching the light
so the broth glows. Appetite first.

Round 1 scored #703 and round 2 scored #513. The subject was right in
both (steaming tonkotsu ramen, noodle pull, jammy egg), but the
execution still trailed the leaderboard top. Round 3 is the steamlit
variant: the same appetite-first subject with stronger light and
atmosphere — warm steam-lit broth, a shining noodle pull, a moodier
frame than the flatter round 1 and the golden-hour round 2.

`main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` byte-for-byte to
`/output/entry.png`. Deterministic, no network, no randomness.
