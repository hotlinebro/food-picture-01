# Tonkotsu Noodle Pull — Round 3 (Variant B, Steamlit)

A photorealistic steaming tonkotsu ramen, steam-lit and moody:
chopsticks lift a tall glossy noodle pull out of creamy broth, a
halved jammy soft-boiled egg shows a runny golden yolk, green onions,
nori, and a speckled ceramic bowl sit in warm light, and the steam
catches the light so the broth glows. Appetite first — the judge
weighs appetite, then texture, styling, light, and craft.

Round 1 scored #703 and round 2 (Variant A, tight 45-degree close-up
with twin jammy eggs, seared chashu, black garlic oil, golden-hour)
scored #513. The subject was right in both rounds — steaming tonkotsu
ramen, noodle pull, jammy egg — but the execution still trailed the
leaderboard top, whose entries pair the same subject with stronger
light and atmosphere. Round 3 is the steamlit variant: the same
appetite-first subject with warm steam-lit broth, a shining noodle
pull, and a moodier frame than the flatter round 1 and the golden-hour
round 2. It follows the sustain-proven top-20 motif (all steaming
tonkotsu ramen with a noodle pull and a jammy egg) and leans harder
into light and atmosphere.

randomness.
